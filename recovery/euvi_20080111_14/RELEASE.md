716 STEREO-A/SECCHI EUVI candidate exposures cover the window from 11 January 2008 at 12:00 UTC to 14 January at 00:00 UTC. The six ZIP files contain the full-resolution mission FITS, grouped by UTC date and wavelength. Each channel has 358 frames. The CSV identifies each frame, its exact archive URL, its wavelength, observation time, exposure duration, size and SHA-256.

The two NPZ files are reconstructed diagnostic sectors in DN/s with time, radius and angle axes. They use the explicit recovery grid and the angle convention documented in the branch README. The original analysis records 357 frames at 171 Å and 358 at 195 Å. Its exact frame selection and the two original polar cubes remain unavailable. This release supplies the observations behind the new reconstruction. The archived 128.21-minute result still requires independent reproduction from the original observable and statistical implementation.

The FITS were retrieved from the [NASA GSFC STEREO Science Center](https://stereo-ssc.nascom.nasa.gov/pub/ins_data/secchi/L0/a/img/euvi/). Source dataset: [STEREO-A/SECCHI/EUVI FITS, DOI 10.48322/260z-fs61](https://doi.org/10.48322/260z-fs61). Please credit the STEREO/SECCHI team. The original compact software release has its own [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22087646).
